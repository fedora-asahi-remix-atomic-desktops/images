# Contributing to this project

## Generative AI / LLM policy

As we are at the intersection of the Fedora and the Asahi projects, we have to
follow the strictest policy from both of those projects to make sure that the
work here can be re-used in either.

The policies are detailed in:

- <https://asahilinux.org/llm-policy/>
- <https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/council/policy/ai-contribution-policy/>

The stictest policy is currently the one from the Asahi project and thus the
one that applies here.
